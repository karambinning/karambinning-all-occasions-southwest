What Wendy makes

A working bench, not a catalogue. Every arrangement is built to the brief — the colours, the budget, the meaning. Below is a sense of the range. Telephone to discuss the rest.

Funeral tributes fresh & lasting

Caskets, sprays, posies and the personal ones — favourite books, the family pet, a teacup, a football team. Discreet collection & delivery across Exeter and the wider Southwest. Talk to Wendy directly.

Wedding flowers bouquet to button-hole

Bridal bouquets, ceremony arrangements, table centres, hair circlets and the button-holes nobody remembers to order until the day before. Consultations welcome at the shop.

Bespoke commissions your idea, our flowers

The Tinkerbell tribute that won Florist of the Week. The "cake-and-tea" piece. The pair of red lips. If you can describe it, Wendy will try and build it — fresh or artificial.

Everyday flowers a little something

Hand-tied wraps for a birthday or a thank you. Walk-in posies. Workplace arrangements on a weekly run. Get-well bunches that don't look like every other get-well bunch.

Artificial florals built to last

For the people who can't have fresh, or want something to keep. Permanent wreaths, year-round arrangements, lasting tributes for the graveside.

One pair of hands.
One shop on Beacon Lane.

All Occasions Southwest is Wendy — a working florist at 88b Beacon Lane, Exeter. Fresh stems sourced for the week, artificial pieces built to last, and the bespoke commissions that other shops politely refuse.

The shop opens Monday to Saturday. Calls are answered between arrangements — leave a message if it rings out and Wendy will get back to you.

Florist of the Week Recognised by Spicer Designs in July 2023 for funeral tribute work — including the Tinkerbell tribute, the cake-and-tea piece and a pair of red lips that landed Wendy a Goody Box and feature spot.
